# Music — an original danza inspired by Juan Morel Campos

- **Study the scores first.** Read the three scores in `refs/music/` and write `music-notes.md` summarizing their shared traits: form (opening *paseo*, then the main sections), meter, the characteristic left-hand rhythm, harmony, cadences, melodic ornament, and tempo character.
- **Compose an original piece** in that style, exactly 60 s long. Do **not** quote or copy any melody from the scores; use them only as style references.
- **Your choices:** the instrumentation (solo piano, or piano with other instruments that suit a danza), the tools and libraries you compose with, and how you render the audio (SoundFonts, sample libraries, synthesis...). Explain your choices in `music-notes.md`. Everything you use must be free and permissively licensed, and installed into `.venv/` or with Homebrew.
- **Shape it to the film:** paseo over the aerial opening, the main melody arriving at Parque de Bombas, a lyrical turn for the cathedral, and a final cadence on the closing crane. Phrase boundaries should land where shots cut.
- **Make it sound played, not sequenced:** expressive dynamics, light rubato, and a light, natural reverb.
- **Deliver** the score as `music.mid` (or another editable source) and the audio as `music.wav` (48 kHz, stereo).
- **Credits:** record every sound source (SoundFont, samples, plugins) with its name, author, source and license in `CREDITS.md`.
