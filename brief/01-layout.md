# Layout

Verify this against the drone frames (`refs/video/frames/`) and the Google Earth screenshots (`refs/photos/01-aerial/`). Where they disagree with this text, they win.

- **Two squares.** The plaza is one city block split into two squares by the cathedral, which stands in the middle. The north square is Plaza Luis Muñoz Rivera; the larger south square is Plaza Federico Degetau.
- **Bounding streets:** Calle Unión on the west, Calle Atocha on the east, and the plaza streets on the north and south.
- **Cathedral orientation:** it runs east–west, with its main facade facing **west**.
- **Parque de Bombas** is attached directly to the cathedral's rear (east end), with its own front facing **east**.
- **Surrounding buildings,** simple but recognizable at the edges of frame: Ponce City Hall (Casa Alcaldía) on the south side, Teatro Fox Delicias on the north, the Armstrong-Poventud residence on the west facing the cathedral, and historic bank buildings on the east.
