# Catedral de Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe

References: `refs/photos/04-cathedral/`, plus the aerial and drone frames for the roof, domes and length.

- Neoclassical, about 42 m long and 19 m wide.
- The west facade has two square three-story towers with windows at every level, each topped by a **silver** dome and a cross.
- Between the towers: tall wooden doors framed by round columns, a large triangular pediment with a large cross on top, and statues at the lower ends of the pediment.
- A large dome over the crossing of the nave and transept.
- Pale exterior (sources describe pale blue and gray). Match the photos for the exact colors.
- Flower beds run along the cathedral's walls (see `brief/07-grounds.md`). Pigeons perch on the pediment (see `brief/07-grounds.md` and shot 3 in `brief/08-camera-and-titles.md`).
