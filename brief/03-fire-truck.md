# The fire truck — Ford Model A "Ponce Engine Co. No. 2"

References: `refs/photos/13-parque-de-bombas-truck-and-interior/` (one photo carries a website logo; ignore it).

It is on display inside the Parque de Bombas museum hall (`brief/02-parque-de-bombas.md`), visible through the open bay, and it is the focus of shot 2 (`brief/08-camera-and-titles.md`). It is a hero asset. Where the photos and this description disagree, the photos win.

---

**Subject: the 1928–31 Ford Model A fire truck "Ponce Engine Co. No. 2" (Parque de Bombas museum, Ponce, Puerto Rico)**

**Overall form**
An open, two-seat fire truck from the late 1920s, built on a Ford Model A chassis, with a fire-equipment body behind the seat. It sits tall and upright and has no windshield, roof or doors. From front to back: bumper, radiator, long hood, short cowl, a raised flat dash panel, an open bench seat, a crosswise copper tank, then a long open-sided rear body carrying ladders. Rough proportions: the hood and cowl make up about a third of the length, the seat and tank area about a quarter, and the rear body the rest. Estimated size is about 14–16 ft long and 5.5–6 ft wide. The top of the searchlight is about 7 ft off the ground. For scale, a stock Model A has a 103.5" wheelbase and a 56" track, and its tires are about 29–30" in diameter.

**Paint and materials**
- **Body:** glossy fire-engine red, slightly warm or orange-red.
- **Lettering and trim:** gold-leaf letters with a black drop shadow. Thin gold pinstripes, often doubled, outline almost every panel and fender edge. Gold scroll or fleur-de-lis ornaments sit in panel corners and on each fender face.
- **Metals:** brass for the bell, searchlight rim and fittings. Chrome or polished nickel for the bumper, headlight rims, hubcaps and radiator cap. Copper for the chemical tank.
- **Other:** black tufted leather seat, natural varnished wood ladders, black tires and frame.

**Front end**
- **Bumper:** two parallel chrome bars, a few inches apart, slightly wider than the radiator. Short vertical end pieces join the bars, with round chrome clamp caps.
- **Radiator shell:** painted red, a tall rectangle with rounded corners. The top edge dips down in a shallow V at the center. A small blue oval Ford emblem sits just below the dip. The grille insert is fine black vertical mesh.
- **Radiator cap:** chrome, with a round temperature-gauge disc on top.
- **Headlights:** two large round lamps with red bowl-shaped housings, chrome rims and clear lenses. They sit on short posts from the fenders. A thin red bar runs straight across the grille between them, at about the height of their centers.
- **Horn:** a black trumpet-style horn with a flared bell, mounted under the driver's-side (left) headlight and pointing forward.
- **Front fenders:** red, deeply curved, sweeping back and down into the running boards. Each has a gold pinstripe border and a gold crest on the front.

**Hood and cowl**
- **Hood:** long, with a rounded top and flat sides. Each side has a framed area of vertical louvers, about 20 or more, in its lower rear section.
- **"PONCE":** large gold block capitals with black drop shadows, running along each hood side above the louvers.
- **Cowl:** a short panel behind the hood. Each side has a gold-bordered rectangle reading "ENGINE / CO. / NO. 2" on three stacked lines, with a gold ornament below.

**Dash and crew area**
- **Dash panel:** flat and upright behind the cowl, rising above the hood line. It has a gold border, gold scroll corners and a small round plaque in the center.
- **Bell:** brass, hanging from a red curved bracket at the passenger-side (right) front corner of the dash.
- **Siren:** a round chrome siren with a slotted face, mounted on top of the dash near the center-right.
- **Searchlight:** a large brass searchlight in a brass yoke, on top of a tall vertical red pole at the driver-side (left) front corner of the seat area. The pole rises from the running board to above head height. A matching red pole stands on the opposite side.
- **Seat:** an open bench in black leather with vertical pleats and rolled, curved side panels. The outside of the seat sides is red with gold scrolls. The steering wheel is on the left.

**Chemical tank and equipment**
- **Tank:** a large copper cylinder directly behind the seat, lying crosswise. Its round flat ends face out on each side, decorated with a gold or brass sunburst design. A copper funnel-shaped filler sits on top.
- **Gauges:** two small round dial gauges and a valve handle on the side panel below and in front of the tank.
- **Top box:** a raised red box with gold pinstripes sits above and behind the seat, higher than the seat back.

**Rear body**
- **Body:** a long red box with flat side panels, each outlined in gold pinstripe with scroll corners.
- **Ladders:** two natural-wood ladders on each side, stacked and lying horizontally on red brackets, running almost the full length of the rear body.
- **Poles:** long wood and brass poles lying along the top edge.
- **Hose:** thick black rubber hose with brass couplings, draped in loose loops along the side from the dash area toward the back.
- **Back end:** a license plate and a small step.

**Running boards and lower body**
- **Running boards:** black, with a brass or gold edge strip, running from the front fender to the rear fender.
- **Tool box:** a red box with chrome latches and gold pinstripes on the driver-side running board, just ahead of the rear fender. A small chrome cylinder stands next to it.
- **Rear fenders:** single-wheel style, red and curved, with gold pinstripes.
- **Frame:** black chassis frame rails show under the body.

**Wheels**
- Four matching wire-spoke wheels, about 30 thin spokes each, with spokes and rims painted red.
- Small chrome Ford hubcaps.
- Single rear wheels, not duals.
- Black tires with block tread, fairly wide for the wheel size.

**Overall character**
A carefully restored parade or museum piece. Every surface is glossy and decorated with gold-leaf pinstriping, so there are bright specular highlights on the red paint, brass and chrome. No dirt or weathering.
