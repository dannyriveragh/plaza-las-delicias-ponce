# Grounds — throughout the plaza

## Hardscape
- Wide mosaic-tile walkways (a real tile pattern with grout lines), marble benches, and ornate 19th-century cast-iron lampposts.

## Planting
- Indian laurel trees (*Ficus microcarpa*) with dense, rounded, neatly trimmed canopies. References: `refs/photos/12-indian-laurel-trees/` and the plaza photos. See **Trees** in `PROMPT.md`.
- Trimmed hedges and flower beds.
- **Flowering beds along the cathedral's walls**, in bloom.

## Pigeons
The plaza's pigeons give it life. Sculpt them as real birds at the camera distances they're seen at, with natural variation in pose and plumage.
- **Morel Campos steps:** a group perched and pecking on the steps of the Juan Morel Campos monument.
- **Cathedral pediment:** a few perched on the pediment; during the cathedral shot, some take off.
- **Fountain rim:** several on the rim of the Fuente de los Leones.
- **Closing flock:** a flock flying through the closing crane shot.

## No people
An empty, sunlit plaza looks intentional.
