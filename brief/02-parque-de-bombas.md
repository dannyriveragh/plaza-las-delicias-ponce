# Parque de Bombas

References: `refs/photos/03-parque-de-bombas/`, `refs/nrhp/` (the National Register file, with the original description and photos), and for the interior `refs/photos/13-parque-de-bombas-truck-and-interior/`.

## Exterior
- A two-story wooden building, about 20 m wide, built as an exhibition pavilion and later used as a firehouse.
- Horizontal wood siding painted in alternating **horizontal** bands of red and black (Ponce's colors).
- A tower at each front corner, flanking a large central space with wide garage-bay openings.
- A pitched roof of galvanized zinc sheets, with decorative curved moldings following the edge of the roof overhang.
- Long, narrow louvered wooden windows, each topped by an oversized fanlight.
- Style: Moorish with Gothic/Victorian touches. Match the photos for trim, signage and exact colors.
