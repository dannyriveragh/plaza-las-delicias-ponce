# Fuente de los Leones

References: `refs/photos/05-fuente-de-los-leones/`, `refs/photos/07-south-plaza-degetau/`, and the drone frames for its position and size.

- The centerpiece of the south square (Plaza Degetau).
- A large round fountain inside a low marble-and-granite wall shaped as a regular octagon, with one corner pointing toward City Hall.
- Four lion statues sit on alternating corners of the octagon. They are hero assets, seen up close in shot 4.
- Animate the water jets and splashing, plus a rippling basin surface (see **Water** in `PROMPT.md`).
- Wet stone around the fountain should be darker and glossier.
- Pigeons perch on the fountain rim (see `brief/07-grounds.md`).
